Privacy, consent, and responsible protection

Privacy & Policy

Aegis Eye Agent is a consent-based device management service for people who want to enroll and protect devices they own or are authorized to manage. It is designed to help an owner find, secure, and manage a lost or stolen enrolled device through an authenticated dashboard.

This policy explains what information may be used to provide the service, how profile pictures help prevent misuse, and the responsibilities that apply to every account holder.

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. A lawful, consent-based service

Aegis Eye Agent is not intended to be spyware, stalkerware, or a tool for secretly monitoring another person. You must not enroll, access, track, record, lock, control, or otherwise manage a device unless you own it or have clear permission and legal authority to do so.

The service is intended for legitimate situations such as protecting your own phone, managing devices assigned to you, recovering a lost device, or administering an organization's devices with appropriate notice and authorization.

We strongly condemn spyware, covert surveillance, harassment, unauthorized access, intimidation, and every other misuse of the service. Accounts or device access may be restricted when activity appears abusive, unlawful, deceptive, or inconsistent with this policy.

2. Information you provide

When you create and use an account, the service may process information needed to provide account access and device-management features, such as:

  • Account details such as your email address, username, phone number, and authentication information.
  • Enrollment details for devices that you choose to connect, including device identifiers, platform information, capabilities, and status information.
  • Subscription and transaction details needed to provide paid plans and account access.
  • Messages or support requests that you voluntarily send through the service.
  • Content and metadata created when you intentionally request supported device actions, such as location results, exported files, live-session information, or recordings.

We aim to collect and use information that is relevant to the feature you request. Do not enter passwords, private keys, access tokens, payment secrets, or unrelated sensitive information into profile fields, device names, messages, or uploaded content.

3. Profile pictures and misuse prevention

The service may ask you to save a profile picture before certain sensitive or paid actions become available. This requirement is a safety and accountability measure intended to discourage abuse and make it harder to operate the service anonymously for harmful purposes.

A profile picture is used as part of your account profile and misuse-prevention controls. Upload only a picture that you have the right to use. Do not upload another person's image without permission, and do not upload intimate, exploitative, deceptive, or unlawful material.

A profile picture does not grant permission to access any device. Device enrollment and every device action still require ownership or authorization, appropriate consent, valid account access, and the device permissions or capabilities required for that action.

We do not describe the profile picture requirement as a guarantee of identity verification. It is one protective signal among the service's account, authorization, and abuse-prevention measures.

4. How device information is used

Device information is used to show your enrolled devices, display status and capabilities, support authorized commands, and help the service respond to requests made from your dashboard. Depending on the device and its permissions, the service may process information such as platform, model, software version, connectivity status, permission status, location results, or device-generated details.

Some features are limited by the operating system, enrollment method, network, device permissions, subscription plan, or device availability. A feature being visible in the interface does not mean that it can bypass platform security, operate without permission, or guarantee recovery.

Live camera, screen, location, file, lock, recording, and owner-message features must be used only for an authorized device and a lawful purpose. You are responsible for providing any notice or consent required by applicable law.

5. Consent and device enrollment

You are responsible for confirming that every device enrolled through your account is yours or is managed with documented authority. Before using a feature that can affect a device or its user, make sure the required consent, notice, workplace policy, family agreement, or legal basis exists.

Do not use the service to secretly monitor a partner, family member, employee, customer, roommate, or stranger. Do not use it to obtain private conversations, images, files, location information, or account information without authorization.

Native device permissions and platform enrollment controls exist for a reason. Do not ask another person to bypass them, and do not attempt to defeat security controls, factory-reset protections, account protections, or operating-system safeguards.

6. Live sessions, location, files, and recordings

When you request a live session, location result, file operation, or recording, the service processes the information needed to complete that request and show its result in your authorized dashboard. Results may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable, or inaccurate because of device state, permissions, connectivity, platform restrictions, or service interruptions.

Live sessions and recordings may contain personal information, images, audio, documents, messages, or other content from the enrolled device. You are responsible for using, sharing, storing, and deleting that content lawfully. Keep access to your account private and do not download or share content unless you have authority to do so.

Do not rely on Aegis Eye Agent as an emergency service, guaranteed theft-recovery service, personal safety system, or replacement for contacting law enforcement, a carrier, an insurer, or another appropriate authority.

7. Security and account responsibility

Protect your login information and sign out of shared devices. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account unless you promptly report unauthorized access and cooperate with reasonable security steps.

Use the service only through legitimate pages and applications. Do not attempt to bypass authentication, subscription controls, authorization checks, rate limits, device permissions, or other safeguards. Do not probe, attack, reverse engineer, or interfere with the service or another user's account.

If you suspect that your account or an enrolled device has been accessed without permission, stop using the affected feature, secure your account, and contact support through the available channel.

8. Sharing and service providers

Information may be handled by service providers that help operate account access, hosting, storage, communication, payments, analytics, security, or customer support. Those providers should receive only the access needed for their role and are expected to handle information according to applicable agreements and law.

We do not sell your profile picture or device information as a product. We do not use the service to provide covert surveillance. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to provide a requested feature, protect users and the service, investigate abuse, comply with law, respond to valid legal process, or protect rights and safety.

9. Retention and deletion

Information is kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain account and device records, to provide requested features, to resolve disputes, to enforce policy, or to meet legal and security obligations. Actual retention can vary by information type and service need.

You may remove saved content through available account controls or request help through support. Deleting content or an account may not immediately remove copies that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, backups, or dispute resolution.

Before deleting an account or device record, make sure you no longer need its enrollment, files, recordings, or recovery information.

10. Children and vulnerable people

The service is not designed to enable secret monitoring of children, partners, employees, or vulnerable people. If you manage a device used by a child or another vulnerable person, obtain the permissions, supervision, notices, and legal authority required in your location, and use the least intrusive feature that meets the legitimate need.

Never use the service to exploit, threaten, control, stalk, or endanger another person.

11. Your rights and questions

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about personal data associated with your account. Requests may require reasonable verification so that we do not disclose information to the wrong person.

Questions about privacy, consent, account information, or suspected misuse should be sent through the support channel available in the portal. Include enough context to help identify the issue, but do not send passwords, tokens, payment secrets, or other credentials.

12. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the service, features, legal requirements, or safety practices change. The latest version will be made available on this page. Continued use after an update means that you have reviewed the updated policy to the extent required by applicable law.

Nothing in this page removes any rights or obligations that cannot be excluded under the law that applies to you. This page is intended to explain the service's privacy and responsible-use approach; it is not a substitute for legal advice tailored to your situation.
