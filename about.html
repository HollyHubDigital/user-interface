About Aegis Eye Agent

Aegis Eye Agent is a user portal for keeping enrolled devices visible, organized, and easier to manage. It brings device access, live sessions, location workflows, files, recordings, and owner message into one focused space for you to access your device if lost or stolen.

Designed for responsible control

The portal is built around clear enrollment, authenticated access, owner-approved actions, and visible device workflows. Availability can vary by device platform, permissions, enrollment state, and subscription plan.

Use Aegis Eye Agent only with devices you own or are authorized to manage. Respect the people who use those devices and the laws that apply where you operate.

One place for your device work

  • Enroll supported devices through the user portal.
  • Review device details and current access status.
  • Request supported live, location, file, lock, and recovery actions.
  • Review and manage saved recordings when the feature is available to your plan.
